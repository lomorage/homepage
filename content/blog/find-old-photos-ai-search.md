You know the photo exists — the kids at the beach, the receipt for the washing machine, the screenshot with the Wi-Fi password. What you don't have is twenty minutes to scroll. Here are the fastest ways to find any photo, and how to do it **without handing your library to a cloud AI**.

<!--more-->

## 1. Describe the scene

Modern AI search understands what's *in* a photo. Search for things like "cat", "beach", "birthday cake" or "snow" and get matching photos, even if you never tagged anything.

## 2. Search by person

Face grouping lets you jump straight to photos of one person — from baby photos to today. It's the quickest way to build a birthday slideshow.

## 3. Search by place

Photos with location data can be explored on a map. Zoom into a city or a holiday spot and see everything you took there.

## 4. Search for words inside images

Screenshots, receipts, documents, menus and signs contain text. Text search inside images finds "invoice", a flight number or a product name in seconds.

## 5. Jump by date

When you remember *roughly when*, browsing by year and month is often fastest.

## Tips for better search results

- **Keep location on in the camera** if you want map browsing (and turn it off for photos you'll share publicly).
- **Back up everything, including screenshots** — they're often the photos you search for most.
- **Import old libraries** so search covers your whole history: use the [Lomorage importer](/docs/Installation/lomorage-importer/) for folders on a drive, or [move out of Google Photos](/blog/2026/11/24/google-photos-alternative/).
- **Clean up duplicates** — fewer near-identical results make searching faster.
