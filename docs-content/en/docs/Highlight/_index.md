---
title: What Lomorage does today
weight: 30
---

Lomorage backs up original photos and videos from phones to storage you control. It supports resumable and incremental transfers, common photo and video formats, Live Photos, family accounts, duplicate and similarity checks, search, sharing, local browsing, imports from existing drives and redundant local copies.

The original files remain in their original format and resolution, organized on ordinary storage rather than locked into a proprietary library.

For the clearest current setup path, start with [Install Lomorage](/docs/Installation/). Features that are still being explored are tracked in the project repositories rather than presented here as available product behavior.
